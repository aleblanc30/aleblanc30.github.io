This is my PhD dissertation !
 
One of the key objectives of neuroscience is to understand animal behavior in terms of its neural basis. We focused on building computational tools to address this question in Drosophila Melanogaster larvae. This work presents techniques to model behavior and quantify the effect of neuronal perturbations. We also extended a model of a circuit for action selection during aversive stimuli to take into account the influence of feeding state. Finally, we describe currently in-progress work to model the locomotor system of the Drosophila larva, a necessary step to further our understanding of motor coordination in this animal.
