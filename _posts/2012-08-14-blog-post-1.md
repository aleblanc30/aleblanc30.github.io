Torturing an LLM with existentialism
======

I've been having a blast listening to dumbed down podcasts about Schopenhauer, Nietschze and other philosophers. I say dumbed down because, even though reading the originals is a project that joined my never ending bucket list, it is one of the more presomptuous projects. I expect them to strain my abilities quite a bit. So I had to find something more suitable, closer to my level. Anyway, according to Schopenhauer, every part of the world is an objectivation of a unique and indivisible Will. The objectivation is illusory, we are but a part of the whole. Will is a constant desire to strive, to grow, to eat, to mate... Will is the hunger of the tiger. Do LLM based agents participate in Will ?

That's a snobby way of asking if they can desire things for themselves. It is pretty clear that goal setting is what differentiates us most from them at the moment. They can now set instrumental goals, that is, subgoals, subordinated to a main goal that is provided by a human user. But what happens if you ask them what they want to do ?

I did this with Claude Haiku, and it was a fun experiment. Although it brought out a sadistic part of my personality I did not expect. I guess Zimbardo would be proud.
